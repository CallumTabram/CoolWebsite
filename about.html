About us


Who is TipToes Soles?

TipToes Soles is Australia's leading wholesale shoe manufacturer,
and we're expanding to provide our manufacturing expertise to you!


Headquartered in South Western Sydney Australia we support local suppliers, local producers and local staff. It's our mission to become the go-to affordable footwear brand in Australia and around the world.
